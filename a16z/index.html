How it runs
  1. 01Flag

    Superposition flags the moments that could have changed the game.

  2. 02Vote

    Fans vote for the moment they want to see, in the broadcaster's app and on socials.

  3. 03Pick

    The fans' moment is picked, and the Predictive Engine measures how close it came.

  4. 04Reveal

    The alternative airs at half-time, visualised with BytePlus via Seedance.

  5. 05Panel

    The panel and fans react. Every vote feeds the next moment.
