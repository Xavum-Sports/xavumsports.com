03PROBA-BALL

Proba-ball by XAVUM

Predicted by XAVUM. Visualised with BytePlus via Seedance. NRL first.

The engagement platform for live sport broadcasts.

Proba-ball takes the heated moments fans argue about and shows the way they could have gone, live in the broadcast. It starts with the NRL and grows across the world’s biggest sports.

  • 03.1Proba-ball momentsPredicts the probable moments in a game and generates them for the broadcast.
  • 03.2Predictive EngineModels sporting outcomes.
03.1AT BROADCAST LEVEL

Re-engage
at halftime.

Watch the moments that didn’t happen.

Proba-ball pulls fans back in where broadcasts usually lose them. Fans vote on the first-half moments they want to see play out differently. XAVUM’s Predictive Engine calls the alternative outcome, visualised with BytePlus via Seedance and revealed during the halftime broadcast.

Historical demonstration: showing how a defining moment could have unfolded differently.

04FAN DEMAND SIGNAL

Fan Demand
Signal.

Every data point. One signal.

A paid reading that tells leagues, broadcasters and sponsors what fans want to see next.

We gather data points wherever fans already are: every vote, pick, reaction, view, share and comment. Together they form the Fan Demand Signal, a measure of fan intent that sits alongside match probability. The Predictive Engine models what is likely to happen. The Fan Demand Signal identifies the moments and matchups fans want to see.
